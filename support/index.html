Support

Write to mayevskydv@gmail.com. Every message gets a reply, usually within two days. If you found a mistake in a rule, a light or a question, please name the screen or quote the question.

I bought the full course but it's locked

Open Today, tap the gear at the top right, then Full course → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. The purchase works on all your iPhones, iPads and Macs.

What happens when the 7-day trial ends?

Nothing is charged and nothing renews. The free lessons stay open, and the rest waits for the one-time unlock. Your progress is kept.

My progress doesn't appear on my other device

Both devices need the same Apple Account, with iCloud allowed for Stand-On (Settings → your name → iCloud). Settings → iCloud in the app shows the sync state. The first sync can take a few minutes.

I can't answer questions in the notification

Long-press the notification, or pull it down, to see the A–D buttons. Settings → Reminders & quick questions → Send me a question now sends a test one.

Can I change the lesson language or the colours?

Yes. Settings → Lesson language: Auto, English, Ukrainian or Russian; the essence of each rule is shown and read aloud in that language. Settings → Appearance: Night, Day or Auto.
