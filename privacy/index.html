Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Stand-On does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of ours that your data goes to.

Your study progress

Your answers, review schedule, lesson records and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they also sync between your devices through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). That database belongs to your Apple Account; we cannot see or access it. You can turn iCloud off for Stand-On in your device settings, and erase all progress in the app under Settings → Progress.

Purchases

The free trial and the full-course purchase go through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details and no information about who you are.

Notifications

Reminders, tips and quick questions are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us. Your answers to quick questions are stored with the rest of your progress.

Children

Stand-On collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
